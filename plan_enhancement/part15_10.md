# Overview

The Administration Workspace serves as the operational backbone of SchoolOS.

It provides a unified environment for managing institutional administration, operational workflows, admissions, finance, transport, library, hostel, procurement, inventory, document management, security, reporting, and automation.

Unlike the Principal Workspace, which focuses on institutional governance and executive decision-making, the Administration Workspace focuses on execution, operational excellence, process automation, and institutional service delivery.

It acts as the Operational Control Center of the Digital School Ecosystem.

---

# Vision

> Empower educational institutions with a fully digital, paperless, automated, secure, and intelligent administration platform capable of managing every operational aspect of modern education.
