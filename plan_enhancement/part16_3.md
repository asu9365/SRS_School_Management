# Overview

The Assignment Management module enables students to manage all academic assignments, projects, research work, presentations, practical tasks, and collaborative activities from a centralized workspace.

Unlike Homework Management, which focuses on daily academic practice, Assignment Management handles long-term academic work involving research, collaboration, milestones, grading rubrics, peer review, presentations, and project-based learning.

Assignments are fully integrated with Student360, Academic Workspace, Examination Center, AI Study Assistant, and Analytics.

---

# Vision

> Build a collaborative assignment ecosystem that develops research, critical thinking, creativity, teamwork, and real-world problem-solving skills.

---

# Objectives

The Assignment Management module aims to

- Centralize assignment management.
- Improve project organization.
- Support collaborative learning.
- Enable milestone tracking.
- Provide transparent grading.
- Improve research quality.
- Encourage peer learning.
- Deliver AI-powered assignment assistance.

# Business Rules

- Every assignment receives a globally unique Assignment ID.
- Assignment deadlines follow teacher publication settings.
- Group assignments track individual member contributions.
- Submission versions remain permanently available for audit purposes.
- Peer reviews remain anonymous when configured by the teacher.
- AI assistance supports learning but does not automatically complete assignments.
- Assignment grades synchronize with Student360 and Gradebook.
- Every assignment activity is audit logged.

# Overview

The Digital Learning module is the intelligent Learning Management System (LMS) within SchoolOS.

It provides students with a personalized digital learning environment that includes eBooks, recorded lectures, live classes, interactive lessons, quizzes, simulations, virtual laboratories, learning paths, AI tutoring, and adaptive learning experiences.

Unlike conventional LMS platforms that simply host content, SchoolOS Digital Learning continuously analyzes student progress and automatically personalizes the learning experience using Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Vision

> Deliver a modern digital learning ecosystem that makes education engaging, personalized, accessible, and available anytime, anywhere.

---

# Objectives

The Digital Learning module aims to

- Centralize learning resources.
- Support hybrid and online learning.
- Improve knowledge retention.
- Enable adaptive learning.
- Increase student engagement.
- Deliver personalized recommendations.
- Support lifelong learning.
- Integrate AI-driven tutoring.

# Business Rules

- Every enrolled student automatically receives access to assigned digital learning resources.
- Learning progress synchronizes across all devices in near real time.
