# Part 13 — Principal Workspace

---

# Overview

The Principal Workspace serves as the Executive Command Center of SchoolOS.

It is designed to provide school leadership with complete visibility into academic performance, student wellbeing, staff productivity, institutional operations, financial health, admissions, compliance, infrastructure, and strategic planning.

Unlike traditional School ERP dashboards that only display administrative statistics, the Principal Workspace provides a real-time, AI-powered executive view of the entire institution.

It enables proactive leadership by transforming operational data into strategic insights.

---

# Vision

> "Empower school leaders with real-time intelligence to make faster, smarter, and evidence-based decisions that improve every student's educational journey."

---

# Objectives

The Principal Workspace aims to

- Monitor overall school performance.
- Improve academic quality.
- Track student success.
- Supervise teacher performance.
- Monitor operational efficiency.
- Strengthen parent engagement.
- Enable AI-assisted decision making.
- Provide complete institutional oversight.

# Overview

The Executive Dashboard is the primary command center for the Principal.

Unlike traditional School ERP dashboards that simply display numbers, the Executive Dashboard provides a real-time operational view of the entire institution, enabling leadership to monitor academic performance, student wellbeing, teacher productivity, financial health, infrastructure, compliance, and AI-driven recommendations from a single interface.

The dashboard is designed around the concept of **"Management by Exception"**, highlighting only what requires immediate attention while providing instant access to deeper analytics.

---

# Vision

> Provide every school leader with a real-time digital twin of the entire institution.

---

# Objectives

The Executive Dashboard aims to

- Provide a complete overview of school operations.
- Monitor institutional performance.
- Detect issues before they escalate.
- Enable rapid executive decision-making.
- Reduce administrative overhead.
- Support AI-assisted governance.
- Centralize strategic information.

# Business Rules

- Dashboard metrics refresh in near real time.
- Critical alerts bypass notification preferences.
- School Health Index recalculates automatically every hour.
- Executive widgets are configurable per principal.
- Every approval action is recorded in the audit log.
- Dashboard personalization is user-specific and does not affect other administrators.
